Where the name comes from.

Fihma comes from fahima, a word meaning to understand, to grasp something deeply. It's the kind of understanding that comes from listening carefully before acting.

That's how we run every engagement. We don't walk in with a packaged solution. We learn your business first: your rhythm, your bottlenecks, the things that slow you down. Then we recommend something.

About Us

Financial discipline meets small business reality.

An AI consulting partner for New York City's owner-operated businesses.

We started Fihma Advisory because we've been on both sides — managing capital at scale and running a business day to day. We know what it costs to do things manually, and we know how to fix it.

The Name

A name with meaning.

Fihma comes from fahima — a word meaning to understand, to comprehend, to grasp something deeply. It carries a sense of wisdom that goes beyond book knowledge. The kind of understanding that comes from listening carefully before acting.

That's the model we bring to every engagement. We don't walk in with a pre-packaged solution. We spend time learning your business — your rhythm, your bottlenecks, the things that slow you down — before recommending anything.

Understanding first. Then action. That's what the name means, and that's how we work.

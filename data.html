Your company records are worth more than you think.

AI companies need real business records to train and test their models: orders, invoices, dispatch logs, service tickets, the paperwork every business piles up. Fihma Data helps owners license those records and get paid for them.

Ask what your data is worth

How it works.

  1. Tell us what you keep

    Which systems you use and how many years of records are in them. We give you a range for what a license could be worth.

  2. We prepare it

    We pull a sample, strip out names, addresses and anything else private, and package it the way buyers expect.

  3. We find the buyer

    We take it to AI companies, negotiate the license, and you sign off before anything is shared.

What owners ask first.

Do I give up my data?

No. You license a copy for a defined use. You keep your records and keep running your business on them.

What about my customers?

Personal and customer identifying details are removed before anything leaves your hands, and you see what's shared before it goes.

What does it cost?

Nothing up front. We earn a share of the license fee when a deal closes.

What kinds of businesses?

Any business with years of consistent records in software. Logistics, field service, healthcare offices, legal and financial firms tend to have the most to offer.
